# Package definitions — user requirements, 2026-11-12
Draft product scope, not a claim that capabilities are implemented. Pricing publication paused pending detailed copy.

## Basic — $79/month
- Free-source U.S. statistical data, macro through local geographies as supported.
- Scout Search included (user-stated standalone price $20/month).
- Included with Market and Enterprise upgrades.
- No paid data feeds authorized; use existing infrastructure/API spend.

## Market — $179 per market per month
- Market: city metro, fewer than 10 jurisdictions within a 30-mile radius; center and exact jurisdiction boundary definition pending.
- Local builders and small companies expanding.
- Permits, jobs, community activity, housing information, floor plans, housing prices, land development, policy changes, economic trends.
- Basic reports and recommendations shared at market level, not company-specific.
- Online platform; Scout included; weekly summaries/updates by email can be included.
- Monthly subscription; unlimited number of markets that may be purchased, not unlimited paid coverage at one market price.
- No export count cap; access restricted to entitled data. Technical anti-abuse controls separate from commercial caps.
- Short signup onboarding asks job type and purpose; choose 3–5 predefined dashboard configurations. User can toggle widgets/data points. Display preferences never grant data access.
- National exploration offers contextual upgrade to deeper research in that market.
- One named user; user requests single concurrent session and sharing prevention. Not yet implemented. Cannot guarantee one human physically uses an account.

## Enterprise — public CTA Book a Call; no public dollar figure
- Discovery call defines company objectives and number of users.
- Custom platform shaped around company needs; not sold per seat.
- Private company data, existing report integrations, custom reports.
- Customer selects specific competitors, niche local data, trends, policy types, housing types, economic metrics.
- Ongoing support (hours and response targets undefined; do not claim 24/7).
- Organization security/admin controls with individually named users; all plans still need sound baseline security.
